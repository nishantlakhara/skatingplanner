# Training Focus & Drill Templates

## Weekly Focus (Rotating)
*   **Week 1: Cross Leg Mastery** - Focus on crossovers, edge control, and lean.
*   **Week 2: Power Starts** - Focus on explosive first 3 steps, arm swing, and low position.
*   **Week 3: Cornering Speed** - Focus on maintaining speed through turns and exit acceleration.
*   **Week 4: Endurance & Technique** - Focus on long laps with consistent form.

## Custom Training Templates (Example)
### Template: High Intensity
*   **Morning:** 15 min mobility, dynamic stretching.
*   **Training:** 2 hours on-ice/on-track, high-speed intervals.
*   **Recovery:** 20 min cool down, foam rolling, protein shake.

### Template: Technique Focus
*   **Morning:** Video analysis of previous session.
*   **Training:** Slow-motion drills, edge work focus.
*   **Recovery:** Yoga/stretching.

### Template: Rest & Mobility
*   **Morning:** Sleep in (9-10 hours).
*   **Training:** None (Active recovery: 20 min walk).
*   **Recovery:** Hydration focus, massage.
