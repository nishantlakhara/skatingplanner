# Nutrition Rules & Meal Templates

## Core Nutrition Guidelines (Vegetarian)
*   **Protein:** Lentils, Paneer, Tofu, Greek Yogurt, Nuts/Seeds.
*   **Calcium:** Milk, Cheese, Broccoli, Almonds.
*   **Carbs:** Oats, Brown Rice, Sweet Potato, Whole Wheat.
*   **Veggies/Fruit:** 5 servings daily (Rainbow colors).

## Nutrition Templates
### Template: Training Day (High Energy)
*   **Breakfast:** Oats with nuts and berries.
*   **Lunch:** Quinoa salad with chickpeas and veggies.
*   **Dinner:** Paneer curry with whole wheat roti and salad.
*   **Snacks:** Fruit, yogurt, protein bar.
*   **Hydration:** 8-10 glasses.

### Template: Competition Day (Fast Fuel)
*   **Pre-Race:** Easily digestible carbs (banana, white toast with honey).
*   **Between Races:** Electrolytes, small fruit snacks.
*   **Post-Race:** Recovery shake, balanced meal.

### Template: Rest Day (Low Intensity)
*   **General:** Focus on micronutrients and fiber.
*   **Hydration:** 6-8 glasses.
